# RED MASS VAMPIRE OS v0.5.2-beta — Darker Voices

RED MASS enters beta with 216 darker voicings for the M-VAVE FM1: muted ritual bells, hollow plucks, haunted choirs, shadowy pads, feral leads and eight sinister grand-piano variations.

## What changed

- 216 of the 256 factory presets have new synthesis settings, envelopes, modulation and effects.
- All 32 bass/808 presets, eight percussion presets and both 16-pad trap kits remain unchanged. Protected preset records and their isolated native audio match v0.5.1 exactly.
- Factory numbers, names, engine assignments and polyphony limits stay the same.
- The firmware, installer and bank metadata identify this release as **v0.5.2-beta**.
- The complete bundle includes the Windows launcher, editor/installer, all 256 audio previews, a 12-pair before/after demo, corresponding source, licences and the previous firmware for rollback.

The six-track layout remains five instrument tracks plus drums on T6, with eight shared voices. Song Mode, performance tools, project formats and MIDI/USB behaviour are unchanged from the preceding sound update. Promotion from alpha to beta does not change the sound data.

## Update notes

Back up your projects, user sounds and samples before updating. After installation, select the desired factory preset again: saved projects retain their previous full sound settings.

This is a **beta / pre-release**. Native preset, audio, import and package checks and the target memory/build checks passed. Physical FM1 installation, real-time timing and sustained load, and execution of the launcher on Windows have not been qualified in this validation. See `docs/TESTING-0.5.2-RU.md` and the validation files in the complete bundle for the scope of the checks.
